\begin{abstract}
For every $\eta>0$ and every sufficiently large integer $N$, there is
a polynomial with $N$ consecutive coefficients in $\{-1,1\}$ whose modulus
lies between $\sqrt N/16$ and $(1+\eta)\sqrt N$ everywhere on the unit
circle.
\end{abstract}
