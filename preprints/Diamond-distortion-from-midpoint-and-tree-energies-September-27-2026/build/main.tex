\begin{abstract}
We derive quantitative distortion bounds for countably branching diamond
graphs from midpoint estimates and direct tree energies. In the dual of
a finite-height segment forest, every distortion-$D$ embedding of the
depth-$k$ diamond satisfies $D^2\ge1+k/4$. The same bound holds in the
infinite-height coordinate predual. We also obtain power-type distortion
bounds for path-cost and recursive tree norms.
\end{abstract}
