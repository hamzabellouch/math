\section{Assembly of the absorption theorems}
\label{sec:assembly}

The preceding construction has used separability, nuclearity, and
condition (C), rather than the canonical-map hypothesis. We now apply
it to the comparison and Cuntz-semigroup criteria in the introduction.

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:condition-C}]
Fix the free ultrafilter in the theorem.
Proposition~\ref{prop:Z-map}, through the support, norm-cone,
tracial-cone, comparison, and packing constructions above, supplies
a unital $*$-homomorphism $\Z\to F_\omega(A)$.
Proposition~\ref{prop:absorption} then gives
$A\cong A\otimes_{\min}\Z$.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{ext:thm:main}]
The zero algebra, if admitted by the convention for simplicity, is
immediate. Nuclearity implies exactness, so
Corollary~\ref{ext:cor:condition-C} shows that $A$ satisfies
condition \textup{(C)}. Theorem~\ref{thm:condition-C} then gives
$A\cong A\otimes_{\min}\Z$.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}[Proof of Corollary~\ref{cor:canonical}]
Again the zero algebra is immediate. Lemma~\ref{lem:C} derives
(C) from the canonical induced map $\Cu(\iota)$, using that
$\Cu(\iota)([b])=[b\otimes1_\Z]$. Theorem~\ref{thm:condition-C}
then proves the assertion.
\end{proof}

\begin{proof}[Proof of Corollary~\ref{cor:abstract-cu}]
The forward implication follows from functoriality of $\Cu$ under
$*$-isomorphisms. For the converse, let
$\Phi:\Cu(A)\to\Cu(A\otimes_{\min}\Z)$ be an abstract isomorphism.
The case $A=0$ is immediate, so put $D=A\otimes_{\min}\Z$ and suppose
$A\ne0$. Then $D$ is nonzero, separable, and nuclear.

Strong self-absorption of $\Z$ supplies an isomorphism
$\varphi:\Z\to\Z\otimes\Z$ approximately unitarily equivalent to
$z\mapsto z\otimes1_\Z$
\cite[Definition~1.3(iv) and Example~1.14(iv)]{TW2007}.
Consequently $\id_A\otimes\varphi:D\to D\otimes\Z$ is an
isomorphism approximately unitarily equivalent to the first-factor
map $j_D(d)=d\otimes1_\Z$. This also holds when $A$ is nonunital:
if $u_n\in\Z\otimes\Z$ are the implementing unitaries, then
$1_{M(A)}\otimes u_n\in M(A\otimes\Z\otimes\Z)$ implement the
equivalence, first on elementary tensors and then on all of $D$ by
density.

Approximately unitarily equivalent homomorphisms induce the same
map on $\Cu$. Indeed, after stabilization, norm approximation by
unitary conjugates gives comparison of every positive cut of either
image with the other image, by
Lemma~\ref{lem:pre:cut-placement}(1); approximate units place the
comparison witnesses in the algebra when the conjugating unitaries
are multipliers. Taking the suprema of cuts in
Equation~\eqref{eq:pre:cuts} gives equality of the two Cuntz classes.
Thus $\Cu(j_D)=\Cu(\id_A\otimes\varphi)$ is an isomorphism.
Lemma~\ref{lem:C}, applied to $D$, gives condition \textup{(C)} on
$\Cu(D)$. Both almost unperforation and the full almost-divisibility
sandwich are intrinsic ordered-semigroup properties, so $\Phi$
transports them to $\Cu(A)$. Theorem~\ref{thm:condition-C} now gives
$A\cong A\otimes_{\min}\Z$.
\end{proof}
