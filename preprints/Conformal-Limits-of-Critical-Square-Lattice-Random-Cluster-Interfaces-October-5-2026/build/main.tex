\begin{abstract}
For every fixed $1\le q\le4$, critical square-lattice random-cluster
Dobrushin interfaces converge to chordal $\SLE_{\kappa(q)}$, where
$\kappa(q)=4\pi/\arccos(-\sqrt q/2)$, and the complete nested plane
loop collections converge to whole-plane $\CLE_{\kappa(q)}$.
The results hold under uniform marked Jordan boundary approximation and
retain loop multiplicities and traversals. At $q=1$ we obtain Cardy's
formula for square-lattice bond percolation with free boundary edges.
\end{abstract}
