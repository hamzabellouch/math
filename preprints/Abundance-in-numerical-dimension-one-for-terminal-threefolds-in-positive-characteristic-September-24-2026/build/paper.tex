\begin{abstract}
Let $X$ be a projective $\Q$-factorial terminal threefold over an
algebraically closed field of characteristic $p>3$. We prove the
numerical-dimension-one case of abundance: if $K_X$ is nef with
$\nu(K_X)=1$, then $K_X$ is semiample and $\kappa(X,K_X)=1$.
\end{abstract}
