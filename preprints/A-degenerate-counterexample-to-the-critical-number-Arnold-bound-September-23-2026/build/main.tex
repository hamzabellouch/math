\begin{abstract}
We construct a smooth Hamiltonian diffeomorphism of the complex quadric
threefold with exactly three fixed points, at least one of which is
degenerate. The critical number and the unit-inclusive rational cup length
of this manifold are both four. Thus the example disproves the unrestricted
critical-number and rational cup-length forms of the Arnold conjecture.
\end{abstract}
