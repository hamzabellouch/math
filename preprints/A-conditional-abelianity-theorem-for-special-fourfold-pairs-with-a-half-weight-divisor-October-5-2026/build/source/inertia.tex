\begin{abstract}
Using five stated results from the companion paper on special compact
K\"ahler manifolds, we prove virtual abelianity of the entire orbifold
fundamental group of the special order-two root orbifold associated with
a pair $(X,\tfrac12D)$, where $X$ is a smooth projective fourfold and $D$
is a nonempty smooth connected divisor. Compatible smooth projective
sources and a dimension minimum give fibrewise adjoint bigness.
Tracking actual meridian orders through the Iitaka fibration then
produces a differential line excluded by specialness. The argument
includes a nontrivial meridian invisible in every finite quotient.
\end{abstract}
