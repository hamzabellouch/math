\begin{abstract}
We prove the weak pinned Erd\H{o}s distinct-distance conjecture.
For every fixed $\varepsilon>0$, all but $o(n)$ points of any
$n$-point planar set determine at least $n^{1-\varepsilon}$ distinct
nonzero distances.
\end{abstract}
