\begin{abstract}
For every fixed integer $s\ge6$, we determine the sharp logarithmic exponent
of the off-diagonal Ramsey number:
\[
 r(s,t)=\frac{t^{s-1}}{(\log t)^{s-2+o(1)}}
 \qquad (t\longrightarrow\infty).
\]
\end{abstract}
