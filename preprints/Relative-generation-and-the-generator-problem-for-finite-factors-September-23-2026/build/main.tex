\begin{abstract}
We prove that every type $\mathrm{II}_1$ factor with separable predual is
generated by one operator, equivalently by two self-adjoint operators.
Combined with the established direct-integral reduction to this case,
this gives an affirmative solution of the generator problem for von
Neumann algebras with separable predual.
\end{abstract}
