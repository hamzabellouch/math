\begin{abstract}
We prove moment estimates for exact random projections of finite measures
whose mass is controlled on Euclidean balls, and derive positive domination
by countable sums of spherical cap measures. For learners with \(M=o(d^2)\)
bits of memory, these estimates give three proofs that uniform-sphere average
success at least \(2/3\) at angular accuracy \(0<\epsilon\le1/10\) requires
\(\Omega(d\log(1/\epsilon))\) noiseless Gaussian observations. The three
proofs keep their different stopping and accuracy costs explicit.
\end{abstract}
