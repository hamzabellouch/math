\begin{abstract}
Let \(A_n\) be a symmetric \(n\times n\) matrix whose entries on and above
the diagonal are independent uniform signs. We prove
\[
 \Pr(\det A_n=0)=\left(\frac12+o(1)\right)^n.
\]
\end{abstract}
