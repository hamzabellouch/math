\begin{abstract}
We prove a pointwise $2$-converse for elliptic curves over $\mathbf Q$
with nonzero rational two-torsion: if the $2^\infty$-Selmer corank is
zero or one, then the analytic rank and Mordell--Weil rank equal that
corank, and the Shafarevich--Tate group is finite. The result allows
arbitrary reduction at $2$.
\end{abstract}
