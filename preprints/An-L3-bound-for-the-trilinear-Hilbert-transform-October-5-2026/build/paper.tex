\begin{abstract}
We prove that the trilinear Hilbert transform with fixed slopes $1,2,3$ maps
$L^3(\R)\times L^3(\R)\times L^3(\R)$ to $L^1(\R)$, resolving this case of
the trilinear Hilbert transform conjecture.
\end{abstract}
