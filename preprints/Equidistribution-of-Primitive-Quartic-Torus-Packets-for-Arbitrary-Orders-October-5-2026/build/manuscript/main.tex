\begin{abstract}
Let $K$ range over totally real quartic fields with no proper intermediate field, and let $\OO$ be any order in $K$. We prove that the packets of periodic diagonal orbits attached to invertible $\OO$-ideal classes, weighted by orbit volume, equidistribute with no escape of mass as $|\Disc(\OO)|$ tends to infinity. The proof combines measure rigidity with a cubic-resolvent estimate that remains uniform at primes dividing the order index.
\end{abstract}
