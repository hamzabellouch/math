\begin{abstract}
We prove a logarithmic window of critical honeycomb bridge lengths: summed through height $2h(\log h)^{1/64}$, bridges of lengths between $h^{4/3}(\log h)^{-1/8}$ and $2h^{4/3}(\log h)^{1/2}$ have mass at least $h^{3/4}(\log h)^{-C}$.
\end{abstract}
