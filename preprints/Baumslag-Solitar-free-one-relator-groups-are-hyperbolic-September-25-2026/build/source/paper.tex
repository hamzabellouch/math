\begin{abstract}
We prove Gersten's conjecture: every finitely generated one-relator group
containing no subgroup isomorphic to a Baumslag--Solitar group
$\operatorname{BS}(m,n)$, for nonzero integers $m,n$, is word-hyperbolic.
\end{abstract}
