# Mathematical inputs and scope

This file records the dependency choice for **Two-step monodromy of special
quasi-projective varieties** (September 24, 2026).

The log-Kodaira-zero corollary uses Campana's direct specialness result
(arXiv version, Theorem 6.7 and Corollary 4.11), followed by the manuscript's
pair-to-open comparison and its stated quasi-Albanese inputs. This replaces
the original corollary's input from the orbifold-Iitaka companion. The compact
companion remains related context; no companion-manuscript theorem dependency
remains in this revision.

The full ordinary-group virtual two-step conjecture remains unresolved. The
canonical completion may have a nontrivial kernel. The surface example
disproves only the auxiliary general-fibre-specialness assertion, not the
ordinary-group conjecture. No full-group result is inferred from compactification
or from bounded representation images.
