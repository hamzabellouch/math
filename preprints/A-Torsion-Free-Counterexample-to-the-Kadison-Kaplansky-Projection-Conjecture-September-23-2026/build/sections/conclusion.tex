\section{Completion of the construction}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorems~\ref{setup:matrix-target}
and~\ref{thm:main}]
Theorem~\ref{spec:matrix-projection} establishes the matrix target of
Theorem~\ref{setup:matrix-target}. It supplies a
finitely generated torsion-free group \(\Gbase\), a finite matrix
size \(N\), and a projection \(p_0\in M_N(C_r^*(\Gbase))\) with
\[
 0<\tau_{\Gbase,N}(p_0)<\frac12.
\]
Now set \(\Gproj=\Gbase*\mathbb F_N\) and apply
Proposition~\ref{scalar:compression}. With
\(U=(\lambda_{\Gproj}(t_1),\ldots,\lambda_{\Gproj}(t_N))\), it gives the
scalar projection
\[
 e=(Up_0)
 (p_0U^*Up_0)^{-1}_{p_0M_N(C_r^*(\Gproj))p_0}
 (Up_0)^*
 \in C_r^*(\Gproj).
\]
The same Proposition proves that \(\Gproj\) is finitely generated
and torsion-free and that
\(\tau_{\Gproj}(e)=\tau_{\Gbase,N}(p_0)\in(0,1/2)\).
Thus \(e\ne0,1\), proving Theorem~\ref{thm:main}.
\end{proof}

The nonintegral trace also places
\([p_0]\in K_0(C_r^*(\Gbase))\) outside the image of the degree-zero
reduced assembly map with trivial coefficients, by
\cite[Proposition~10.3]{BGW2016}.
