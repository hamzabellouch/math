\begin{abstract}
We prove a mod-$\ell$ Bogomolov--Pop reconstruction theorem for function
fields of transcendence degree at least two over arbitrary algebraically
closed fields of characteristic different from $\ell$. The groups
$K^{\mathrm M}_1/\ell$ and $K^{\mathrm M}_2/\ell$, together with their full
bilinear product, determine the perfect closure and its constant field.
Every compatible isomorphism of these data is induced by a field isomorphism
up to a single scalar in $\F_\ell^\times$, with only Frobenius ambiguity in
the field isomorphism in positive characteristic.
\end{abstract}
