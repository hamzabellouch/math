# Mathematical input and scope

This manuscript concerns Gaboriau's general fixed-price problem for countably
infinite groups, using a rank-100 amalgam construction. This is distinct from
the fixed-price-one question restricted to groups with property (T).

The manuscript was developed from an intermediate source argument. The
available source record does not establish that it was the final revision of
that argument. The article and its mathematical claims are listed in
[README.md](README.md).
