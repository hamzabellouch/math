\begin{abstract}
We determine the sharp logarithmic exponent of the off-diagonal Ramsey
number \(r(5,t)\):
\[
 r(5,t)=\frac{t^4}{(\log t)^{3+o(1)}}
 \qquad (t\longrightarrow\infty).
\]
\end{abstract}
